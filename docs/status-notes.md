# Status notes

Write one to three lines about what you are working on, in your own words.
The status agent reads this file, because commits alone do not show your plans.
Keep it short and update it when your focus changes.

- Goal: (one sentence: what is this project for?)
- Working on: (what you are doing right now)
- Blocked by: (anything stopping you, or "nothing")
- Next: (what you plan to do after this)
